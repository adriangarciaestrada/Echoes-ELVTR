# Lore Bible: Cosmology of the Architects — Echoes (GDD V2)

## World Backstory
The Architects were an ancient, non-human civilization that wove reality using luminous energy threads ("The Weave"). They left behind colossal stone and metal structures, now silent and decaying. Remnants of their automated defense constructs still haunt the corridors.

## Narrative Tone
Sci-fi melancholic, ancient architectural mystery, cryptic yet diegetically grounded.

## Fragment Categories
1. **Murals:** Carved stone inscriptions detailing the arrival of the Architects and the creation of the Beacons.
2. **Data Terminals:** Sci-fi logs recording the onset of decay and the sealing of La Costurera.
3. **Class Echoes:** Lore nodes unique to each branch explaining the origin of the Hunter's Grapple Knife and Titan's Charge Bash.
