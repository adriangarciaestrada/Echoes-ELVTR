# Boss Specification: La Costurera — Echoes (GDD V2)

## Encounter Identity & Concept
Alien witch commanding two revived knights. She fights at range with energy volleys and beams while her knights maintain melee pressure.

## Core Punish Mechanism & Synchronized Kills
- **Invulnerability Shield:** La Costurera is 100% invulnerable while EITHER knight stands.
- **Revive Weave Timer:** When Knight 1 falls, she begins *re-stitching* him — a visible luminous weave rebuilding his body. The weave **is** the timer until Knight 1 revives at full health.
- **Punish Window:** The player MUST drop Knight 2 fast enough so BOTH knights are downed simultaneously. Only when BOTH knights are down is La Costurera grounded, channeling, and **vulnerable to damage**.
- **Persistent Damage:** Damage dealt to La Costurera inside punish windows accumulates across windows until she dies (resets only on player death). Grenades/shots landing outside punish windows deal 0 damage.
