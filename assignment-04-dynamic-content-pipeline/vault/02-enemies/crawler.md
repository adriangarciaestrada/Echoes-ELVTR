# Archetype 1: Crawler (Melee Swarm) — Echoes (GDD V2)

- **Role:** Fast pursuer / swarm unit.
- **Behavior:** Follows floors, walls, and ceilings. Closes distance rapidly to force player movement.
- **Stats (`DT_EnemyStats`):** Low HP (1-2 hits to die). Low damage per hit.
- **Combat Purpose:** Creates room friction; interrupts sloppy movement; forces player to fire while backpedaling or jumping.
