# The Architects — cosmology and tone

## The world

The Architects were an ancient, non-human civilisation that wove reality
using luminous energy threads (**the Weave**). They left behind colossal
stone and metal structures, now silent and decaying. Remnants of their
automated defence constructs still haunt the corridors.

## Narrative tone

**Sci-fi melancholic, ancient architectural mystery, cryptic yet
diegetically grounded.**

Consequences for every line of text in this game: no cheerfulness, no
exclamation marks, no congratulating the player, no addressing them as a
player or as a gamer, no marketing register, no modern casual idiom. The
text speaks from inside the world or not at all.

## What the player is

A **Weaver** — the class lore skin. The thing defended is a **Beacon**, an
Architect anchor. What descends the lane are **Remnants**.
