# Archetype 5: Blink Tank (Teleport Heavy) — Echoes (GDD V2)

- **Role:** Teleporting heavy pressure unit.
- **Behavior:** Takes initial damage, then teleports behind the player to strike. High sustained tracking.
- **Stats (`DT_EnemyStats`):** High HP (8-12 hits to die). Heavy melee damage.
- **Asymmetric Impact:** Punishes Hunter's dodge reliance (teleport shrinks dodge escape window); tests Titan's sustained frontal blocking shield.
