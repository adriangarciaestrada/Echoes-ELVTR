# Archetype 3: Shieldbearer (Chokepoint Wall) — Echoes (GDD V2)

- **Role:** Frontal wall / chokepoint blocker.
- **Behavior:** Carries a full-height frontal invulnerable energy shield + heavy melee knockback attack when approached.
- **Placement Rule:** MUST leave >= 300u vertical clearance overhead (for Hunter hop/grapple) AND >= 400u horizontal runway in front (for Titan charge bash).
- **Asymmetric Solution:**
  - **Hunter:** Hopped over via double jump or grapple knife anchor.
  - **Titan:** Broken through via Charge Bash wall-breaker.
