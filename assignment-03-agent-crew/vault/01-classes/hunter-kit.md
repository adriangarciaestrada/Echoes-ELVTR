# Class Specification: Hunter (P0) — Echoes (GDD V2)

## Identity & Combat Dialect
Agile, high-mobility precision class. Mobility is Hunter's defense. The full kit is open from minute one.

## Verb Mapping & Mechanics

| Button Category | Action Verb | Mechanics & Tunables (`DT_PlayerFeel`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Jump** | **Double Jump** | Vertical reach ~450 units. Instant turnaround, variable jump height (~40% min). |
| **Defense / Dodge** | **Dodge (i-frames)** | Invulnerability-frame roll/dash (400 ms total, 250 ms core i-frames). Brief recovery beat — no frame-one spam. Defense has priority (fire cancelable to dodge). |
| **Traversal Tool** | **Grapple Knife** | Grappling chain knife. Anchors to ceiling/wall grapple points within 800u. Opens Hunter-exclusive Segment B gates. |
| **Fire** | **Semi-Auto Hand Cannon** | High precision, semi-automatic ranged fire. Instant turnaround, zero landing lag. |
| **Grenade** | **Sticky Burst Grenade** | Sticks to target surface/enemy and bursts after a beat. Rewards banking ahead of boss punish window. Flat cooldown. |

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
- Low damage absorption. Punished heavily by compressed safe ground and predictive area-denial volleys (La Costurera's primary tactic against Hunter).
