# Boss Adaptive Intelligence & Tactics — Echoes (GDD V2)

## Class-Specific Adaptation Rules
La Costurera's squad adapts its pressure by pattern rather than raw numbers:

### Against Hunter
- **La Costurera's Tactic:** Attacks sharpen into predictive ranged volleys. Covers player's favorite dodge direction based on dodge frequency tracking. Area denial beams compress safe ground.
- **Knights' Tactic:** Push Hunter toward arena corners.

### Against Titan
- **La Costurera's Tactic:** Directs knights to maintain split-angle flanking attacks.
- **Knights' Tactic:** Pincer positioning and staggered melee swings that Titan's frontal shield cannot cover simultaneously. Forces Titan to trade health (health drops sustain Titan).
