# Archetype 4: Walking Bomb (Proximity Explosion) — Echoes (GDD V2)

- **Role:** Proximity explosive unit.
- **Behavior:** Slow moving, volatile ground unit that rushes when player enters detection radius and detonates on contact or death after brief fuse beat.
- **Stats (`DT_EnemyStats`):** Low HP (1 hit to die). Massive AoE damage on explosion.
- **Asymmetric Impact:** Punishes close-quarters play (Titan); trivial for ranged agile play (Hunter).
