# Class Specification: Titan (P1 Stretch) — Echoes (GDD V2)

## Identity & Combat Dialect
Heavyweight, frontline brawler class. Position retention and damage mitigation define Titan. The full kit is open from minute one.

## Verb Mapping & Mechanics

| Button Category | Action Verb | Mechanics & Tunables (`DT_PlayerFeel`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Jump** | **Sustained Lift** | Directional vertical ascension (~600 units reach). High momentum, lower agility. |
| **Defense / Dodge** | **Absorbing Shield** | Frontal directional barrier. Depleting energy pool (meter visible on HUD). Blocks incoming frontal damage; does not provide 360° i-frame immunity. |
| **Traversal Tool** | **Charge Bash** | Wall-breaking horizontal shoulder bash. Destroys cracked barriers and staggers heavy enemies. Opens Titan-exclusive Segment B gates. |
| **Fire** | **Auto Rifle** | Automatic sustained automatic fire. High suppression, spread over distance. |
| **Grenade** | **Area Sustained Pulse** | Opens a sustained damage zone on the ground with heavy offensive weight. Flat cooldown. |

## Weaknesses & Vulnerabilities
- Slower movement and lower dodge agility. Punished by flanking melee attacks (La Costurera's knights flanking tactic) and proximity bombers. Trading health is budgeted via enemy health drops.
