# Boss Vanguard: Revived Knights — Echoes (GDD V2)

## Unit Specifications
- **Assets:** Two humanoid knights sharing scaled knight rig models (cost-optimized).
- **Combat Function:** Primary melee pressure and player tracking.
- **Revive Cycle:** When downed, knight enters "Weave State" (luminous thread VFX). Takes ~8-12 seconds for weave to complete. If second knight is downed during this window, both stay down and Witch becomes vulnerable.
- **Tactics:**
  - **Against Hunter:** Corner hunter against arena walls to compress dodge space.
  - **Against Titan:** Execute pincer flanking attacks (one front, one rear) to bypass frontal energy shield.
