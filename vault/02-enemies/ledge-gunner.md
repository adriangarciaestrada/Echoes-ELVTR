# Archetype 2: Ledge Gunner (Elevated Shooter) — Echoes (GDD V2)

- **Role:** Elevated ranged shooter.
- **Behavior:** Fixed to ledges, platforms, or high alcoves. Fires direct line-of-sight energy projectiles at regular intervals.
- **Stats (`DT_EnemyStats`):** Medium HP (3-4 hits to die). Medium damage.
- **Combat Purpose:** Calibration unit for TTK (Time-To-Kill); forces player to cover or timed vertical jump approach.
